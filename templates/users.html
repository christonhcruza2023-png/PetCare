Permission reference

  • Administrators — create, edit and delete pet records, upload photos, and manage accounts.
  • Viewers — read-only. They can browse the dashboard, search records and open pet profiles, but cannot change anything.

The first account created on a fresh install becomes an administrator. At least one administrator must always remain, so the last one cannot be demoted or deleted.
